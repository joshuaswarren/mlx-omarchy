## What shipped

1. **SHA-256 hardware payload verification** (`0986add5`): `bundle.cpp`
   dispatches `sha256_compress` to the ARMv8 crypto extension at runtime
   (`getauxval` HWCAP_SHA256, canonical sequence, scalar fallback
   unchanged). Digests identical by construction; evidenced below by KATs
   and a compiled HWCAP probe against the deployed libmlx on both hosts.
2. **libmlx identity guard** (`88a0bc71`): the encoder runner resolves the
   mapped libmlx from `/proc/self/maps`, records path+sha256+dist
   version, and hard-fails on mismatch.
3. **Collector data-integrity fixes + macOS capture upgrade** (the v0.6.5
   work, now on `main`), worker schema unchanged in identity terms:
   `schema_version` 1, `schema_sha256` `0efcb8dc…`, `fields_sha256`
   `ec757565…`.

## RED that was the harness, not the product (environmental/product split)

The first driver run on both hosts went RED on the ctx digest check with
`ff502900d2a179a5`. Root cause: my digest parser matched legs with
`endswith("longctx-1024-decode-32")`, which also matched the
`qwen25-0.5b-bf16:longctx-1024-decode-32` arm (`ff502900d2a179a5` — a
different model precision, correctly a different digest) and overwrote the
4bit row. The fresh 4bit legs in the same JSON carried the pinned digests
all along. Fixed to exact leg-id match and re-run end to end: GREEN. The
wheels were never in question and the release stayed draft throughout.
