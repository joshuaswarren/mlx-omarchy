# 2026-09-14 LSTM emission-101: the divergent step named

Status: named, not closed. The recurrent divergence is the two unary
evaluations (`sigmoid`, `tanh`) inside the `lstm` op — the fp16 values
consumed by the products that form `next_cell` and `next_hidden` per layer —
not the gate reduction and not the state algebra.

- Hole: `parakeet.tdt.decoder-lstm.fp16-recurrent-vs-native-coreml`.
- Named step: elementwise activation evaluation inside the recurrent op.
- Named tensors: `next_cell[layer]` and `next_hidden[layer]`, layer 0 and 1,
  through the unary outputs consumed forming them.
- Excluded as the divergence: the gate reduction (the reduction-free step
  still misses 227 of 640 lanes), the state algebra and product width (the
  host model reproduces the shipped GPU step bit for bit, 185600 of 185600
  lanes, and no algebra variant exceeds 413 of 640 at the reduction-free
  step), the projector and joint (closed in the earlier receipts), and the
  encoder (the run uses the macOS capture's own `encoder_hidden`).
- The elementwise closure `c9f78a9c` adopted the Mac CPU rerun contract
  (correctly rounded fp16). This receipt measures that the capture defining
  the native golden disagrees with that contract inside the recurrence: the
  hole's content is the native unary function, still unidentified, consistent
  with the activation-stages receipt's refutation counts.

Token-exact E2E is not claimed. No shipped code changed. `63c1d3cf` not
merged. jw16 untouched.

## The reduction-free step has no algebra left to blame

Transition 0 layer 0: the all-zero embedding row and zero entry state make
every gate exactly the fp16 bias constant under every reduction candidate.
Scoring `f16(S_f·c + S_i·T_g)` against the captured `decoder_next_cell[0,0]`
over unary materialisation x product width:

| sigmoid feed | tanh feed | product | lanes of 640 | max abs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| r16 | r16 | p32 | 413 | 0.0009765625 |
| r16 | r16 | p64 | 413 | 0.0009765625 |
| r16 | f32 | p32/p64 | 366 | 0.0009765625 |
| f32 | f32 | p32/p64 | 349 | 0.00048828125 |

Widening the feeds only worsens it. The native consumes fp16-rounded unary
values and one fp16 multiply — the shape the stages receipt settled — but the
values are not correct rounding: 227 of 640 lanes sit ±1 fp16 ulp off with a
symmetric histogram. The same holds for `next_hidden[0,0]` (best 291 of 640).
No product rounding, no feed width, no state-algebra placement reaches past
413; the residual is upstream of the algebra, in the unary values themselves.

Over all 15 comparable transitions, native-injected state each time, the best
recurrence contract (`fp32-dots-fp32-add-bias-fp16`) scores 3382 of 19200
`next_cell` and 3193 of 19200 `next_hidden` lanes; the shipped contract
scores 566 of 1280 cell lanes at transition 0 and 156–230 of 1280 once real
reductions run. The gate reduction carries a second-order residual on top of
the unary residual; it is not the first divergence.

## The live step is the shipped algebra, exactly

The `shipped` arm's spy records the GPU gate args (`sequence[0] @ W_ih.T +
hidden @ W_hh.T + bias`, fp16) for all 145 calls. Recomputing every step
host-side from those gate args — correctly-rounded unaries, fp16 products,
fp16 add, fp16 cell feeding `tanh` — reproduces the recorded GPU tensors
bit for bit:

| tensor | lanes bit-exact |
| --- | --- |
| `next_cell`, all calls, both layers | 185600 of 185600 |
| `next_hidden`, all calls, both layers | 185600 of 185600 |

The shipped recurrence contains nothing but the unaries and the fp16
algebra. Since the algebra is exact and the reduction-free step still
misses, the divergence the live decode accumulates is the unary evaluation.

## The emission-101 miss is that residual, accumulated

Native emissions 96..103 at frame 373, duration 0:
`7883, 7883, 7883, 7863, 8135, 7892, 7863, 8135`. With the encoder fixed to
the macOS capture's own hidden, the shipped arm reproduces the tdt-emission-99
receipt's native-encoder arm bit for bit (e99 `logit_7863 = -14.1484375`,
identical across runs) and first misses at 101:

| emission | argmax | logit argmax | logit native token | gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 99 | 7863 | -14.1484375 | -14.1484375 | 0 |
| 100 | 8135 | -16.921875 | -16.921875 | 0 |
| 101 | **8029** | -12.6875 | 7892: -12.921875 | 0.234375 |

The chain-sigmoid arm misses at the same emission with the same token, and
that is the attribution measurement: substituting one known-better unary
function on the same gate args changes on average 194 `next_cell` and 248
`next_hidden` lanes of 640 per call-layer, moves the emission-101
`decoder_state` by max_abs 0.009765625 (rel_l2 0.0031), and moves the e101
argmax logit from -12.6875 to -12.5859375 — a 0.10 shift against a 0.234
flip margin, from the sigmoid half alone. Per-step 1-to-2-ulp unary
residuals on roughly a third of lanes, over 145 decoder calls, produce
logit drift of the same order as the margins at frame 373; the accumulated
residual crosses one at 101.

## Why not the alternatives

- Gate reduction: the reduction-free step misses 227 of 640 lanes with the
  gates equal to the bias constants bit for bit.
- State algebra / product rounding: the host model of the shipped step is
  185600 of 185600 against the recorded GPU tensors, and the transition-0
  grid caps at 413 of 640 across every feed and product width.
- Projector / joint: projector closed bit-exact on all 9600 captured lanes;
  the e99 swaps showed the joint follows `decoder_state`, not the frame.
- Encoder: this run uses the macOS capture's `encoder_hidden`; the encoder
  residual is absent by construction, and 99–100 still match.
- `8289b1bc`-style widening: refuted on measurement in the semantics
  receipt; wider feeds also worsen the reduction-free grid here.
