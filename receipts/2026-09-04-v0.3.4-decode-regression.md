# v0.3.4 decode regression: 4-bit 12.5 -> 0.21 tok/s, fixed in v0.3.5

Machine: jwm1, Apple M1 (G13G B1), Honeykrisp Vulkan 1.4.354.
Model: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, 4-bit and bf16. Pinned 64-token generation,
EOS suppressed, decode reported over the final 63 tokens, prefill separate.
Harness: `scripts/bench_decode.py` at 0535e62; every run prints the
provenance line and refused to emit a number unless the loaded `libmlx.so`
matched the installed wheel. Fresh venv and fresh process per arm.

## What shipped

v0.3.4 was tagged at d687505 = 0529140 (the measured commit) plus a
cherry-pick of the hang watchdog (d6f6bf2). The watchdog replaced the
blocking `vkWaitSemaphores` in `wait_for_timeline_progress` with
`sleep_for(100 ms)` followed by a counter read, in a loop. No blocking
wait remained, so every host wait on GPU completion cost up to a full
tick instead of returning when the signal landed. At about 48 waits per
decode token that is 4.8 s/token.

Nobody measured decode on the wheel that was uploaded. The release
notes' 12.52 tok/s was measured at 5ac59e8, before the watchdog; the
release gate checks hashes, platforms and feature strings and none of
that sees speed; the watchdog's own batteries passed on llvmpipe, where
execution is synchronous and no wait ever outlives a tick; and its
hardware receipt covered the wedge case and a 6,009-token chunked run
that completed without anyone asking how fast.

## The fix

- b4aed84: wait on the semaphore with the poll interval as the timeout.
  A completed signal wakes the caller at once; only a wait that outlives
  the interval reads the counter to tell slow from stalled.
- 0535e62: drop the counter read that preceded every wait. It was a
  driver call paid on every wait for nothing.

Hang detection is unchanged: the timeline counter must advance within
`kSubmitHangNoProgressNs` (10 s) or the wait throws by name.

## Three arms, one session (2026-09-04 09:24 to 09:57)

Arm 1 = 0529140 (the commit before the watchdog), sha256 b465005f.
Arm 2 = d687505, the published v0.3.4 aarch64 asset, sha256 869ffe3f.
Arm 3 = 0535e62, the fix, sha256 f55241d1.

4-bit decode, tok/s, five runs each:

| arm | r1 | r2 | r3 | r4 | r5 | median | prefill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 pre-watchdog | 12.54 | 12.52 | 12.43 | 12.67 | 12.51 | 12.52 | 1.19 s |
| 2 shipped v0.3.4 | 0.21 | 0.21 | 0.21 | 0.21 | 0.21 | 0.21 | 19.45 s |
| 3 fix | 12.49 | 12.65 | 12.57 | 12.61 | 12.69 | 12.61 | 1.19 s |

bf16 decode, eager (`MLX_DISABLE_COMPILE=1`; bf16 compiled tapes are
gated on Honeykrisp), tok/s:

| arm | r1 | r2 | r3 | r4 | r5 | median | prefill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 pre-watchdog | 8.73 | 8.75 | 8.68 | 8.79 | 8.76 | 8.75 | 0.60 s |
| 2 shipped v0.3.4 | 0.21 | - | - | - | - | 0.21 | 19.45 s |
| 3 fix | 8.76 | 8.72 | 8.74 | 8.75 | 8.79 | 8.75 | 0.59 s |

Arm 2 bf16 ran once: the 4-bit arm had already shown the wheel is unusable.

The fix recovers the full regression. A 15% gap reported earlier
(12.39 on the fix vs 14.56 on 0529140) was two sessions hours apart on
a box that had been building between them; measured in one session the
arms are within run noise.

## Wedge detection preserved

On arm 3, a single 2,048-token eval in a fresh process raised
`[omarchy] Vulkan timeline counter failed to advance for 10000 ms (last
observed=0, target=1)`. The watchdog still does the one thing it was
for.

Logs: jwm1:`~/benchq/logs/3arm/` and `~/benchq/logs/3arm-session.log`.

## Process

Four agent reports in one night claimed work was running when the box
showed load 0.00 and no process. The liveness procedure (check `ps` and
newest artifact before waiting) is now mandatory for anything queued
behind a background job, and a release now carries one decode number
measured on the uploaded asset (`docs/release.md`, step 5).
