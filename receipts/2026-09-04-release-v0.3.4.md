## What v0.3.4 ships

- **Fused RoPE kernel.** Decode dispatches per token fell from 1,868.7 to
  715.5 (-61.7%) and submissions from 95.2 to 48.1 (-49.5%) on the 4-bit
  Honeykrisp path; 4-bit decode rises from 7.16 to 12.52 tok/s (+74.9%),
  bf16 decode from 6.81 to 8.75 tok/s (+28.5%), bf16 prefill 49.8 -> 60.8
  tok/s, 4-bit prefill 27.4 -> 30.2 tok/s. Two RoPE variants are fenced
  to the composed path (per-batch vector offsets, the inverse / VJP
  path) because they have not passed equivalence. Full pair, mechanism,
  and the three-arm v0.3.2 / `7c25feb` / v0.3.3 column are in
  `receipts/2026-09-03-dispatcher-compile-and-column-replace.md` (the
  "Fused RoPE on hardware" and "Three-arm pinned re-measure" sections).
- **Compiled tapes re-enabled by default.** The v0.3.3 root cause was a
  stale traced shape on shapeless tape reuse (prefill-traced fragment
  served a decode call whose input was a different shape; node outputs
  kept the prefill sizes; decode read past its buffers). The fix
  `26c6714` re-derives every node's shape at eval time from
  `primitive().output_shapes(real_inputs)`; the re-enable `dfe8f17`
  retires the auto-eager device gate. Compiled runs on Honeykrisp are
  now correct AND within 2% of eager (7.10 vs 7.25 tok/s, 5/5 compiled
  runs, no auto-eager warning). Full root cause and proof:
  `receipts/2026-09-03-stale-shape-tape-corruption.md`.
- **No-progress watchdog (submit deadline).** A 10 s wall-clock cap
  misdiagnosed long legitimate work as a hang. The deadline is now a
  no-progress detector that polls `GetSemaphoreCounterValue` every 100
  ms and declares a hang when no counter advance is observed across
  `kSubmitHangNoProgressNs` (default 10 s, overridable via
  `MLX_OMARCHY_HANG_NO_PROGRESS_NS`). A separate `kSubmitMaxWallNs`
  (default 30 minutes) caps total wall time. A real hung device stalls
  the counter and trips the watchdog in the same 10 s window.
- **Provenance gate from v0.3.3 carries forward.** Wheels record their
  source commit in the version's local segment; the bench and the data
  collectors refuse to emit numbers when the loaded `libmlx.so` does
  not match the installed wheel; the new
  `scripts/verify-release-assets.py` is what verified this release
  (below).
