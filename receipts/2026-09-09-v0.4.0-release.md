## Out-of-box install, stock Mesa

`curl -fsSL .../main/install.sh | bash` run in a fresh `$HOME` on the M1
against the stock asahi-alarm Mesa 26.1.7 Honeykrisp driver (the fork build
installed on the box was bypassed with `VK_DRIVER_FILES` pointing at the
stock `libvulkan_asahi.so` extracted from the pacman cache): packages, wheel
download and checksum, venv, launchers, `.desktop` entry, smoke test
`device: Apple M1 (G13G B1)` / `matmul OK`. `mlx-omarchy-demo --once` then
streamed the scripted answer at 76.3 generated tok/s.
`install.sh --uninstall` left `~/.local/share/mlx-omarchy`, the two
launchers, and the desktop entry removed.
